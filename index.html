Qualifications & standards

What it takes to rent with us.

Clear and up front, and the same for everyone — so you know exactly where you stand before you apply. And because we own the properties, you can talk it through with the person who actually makes the decision.

Income & employment

  • Gross monthly income of at least 3× the rent for approval
  • Co-signers / combined income must be 4× the rent
  • Gainfully employed & verifiable — your two most recent paystubs (or proof of income from other sources)
  • Self-employed applicants: most recent tax records may be required
  • Valid government-issued photo ID · applicants must be 18+

Application & deposit

  • $50 per-person application/set-up fee — charged only if you're approved (no charge if not)
  • Security deposit equal to one month's rent
  • With credit challenges, the deposit may be 1.5× the rent
  • First month's rent + deposit (plus any pet fee) due at lease signing

Pet policy

  • All pets must be approved by management (any pet or breed may be declined)
  • Maximum of 2 pets per household
  • Pet fee: $350 for the first pet, $250 per additional pet (non-refundable)

Lease terms

  • 12-month minimum lease — month-to-month is not available
  • A full 60-day written notice is required before moving out
  • Early termination requires 60-day notice plus rent for the remaining term

Meet the criteria? Apply online — or reach out with questions first.

Resident resources

Everything for a smooth move.

A quick guide for new and current residents across Conway, Myrtle Beach & Socastee — and we're always a call away at 301-542-2542.

Moving in

At signing, your first month's rent and security deposit (plus any pet fee) are due. Then we do a move-in walkthrough together and hand over the keys.

Utilities & setup

Set up power, water, and internet before move-in. In our areas that's usually Santee Cooper or Horry Electric, Grand Strand Water & Sewer (or the City of Conway), and Spectrum or HTC. We'll confirm the right ones for your unit.

Renters insurance

We recommend every resident carry renters insurance — it's usually $10–20 a month and protects your belongings if something happens. Ask us if you'd like a referral.

Moving out

Give us a full 60-day written notice, leave the place clean and as you found it, and we'll handle your security deposit return per the terms of your lease.
